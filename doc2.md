What would the client have asked a vendor for, in writing?
Owner: Client Lead. Closes: ladder stage 2. Length: one to two pages.
You write this request for proposal as the client would have issued it, so that both sides agree on what is being bought before anything is built. Vendor teams often draft the request with the client in B2B sales; treat that as the convention here. Keep the client's voice and the client's units: rooms, euros, walks.
1. What is the background, in three sentences?
Our answer:
There are two hotels group in Portugal one in Lebanon and other in Algarve. Across the group there, about one booking in four is cancelled, and cancelled room that the hotel fails to resell earns nothing that night. Revenue manager oversells each night with a flat rule: the hotel's average cancellation rate times the night's bookings, less a few rooms for safety.

2. What must the solution do?
Our answer:
(1)	Give the exact number of rooms that the hotel can oversell to compensate their loss due to cancellation or no show by the guests.
(2)	To get the maximum profit out of their empty rooms by the model.

3. How good must it be, and on which period?
Our answer:
The cancellation rate in the period from July 2015 to August 2017 of the Resort Hotel in Algarve was around one booking in four. And for the city hotel it was 41.73% and combining both at 37%. 

4. Within which limits and rules?
Our answer:
Using the data coming from the bookings table to get all the necessary information about the customers to get the no. of cancellation and provide an overselling on that night.

5. What data do we provide?
Our answer:
 A table showing for each of the fourteen nights at each hotel and how many to oversell.

6. How will we judge proposals?
Our answer:
A room left empty by a cancellation costs the hotel what the guest had booked. A guest who arrives in a full hotel is walked to another hotel by the hotel’s expense which costs EUR 300 per guest to the hotel. The hotel brand standard is at most 10 walked guest per 1000 arrivals.

7. What is the timeline?
Our answer:
Training on arrivals before 1 July 2016, validating in July to December 2016, and prove rule once in January to August 2017.

8. What may the vendor ask us?
Our answer:
 About how many there would be due to the cancellation and no show respectively; which distribution channel the hotel should target to get maximum bookings; also, how many are the repeating guest coming to them and get better understanding of the market.

9. What is on the wish list?
Our answer:
Marketing team would love to find the hidden guest types.
